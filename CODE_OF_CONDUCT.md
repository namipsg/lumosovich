# Code of Conduct

Lumosovich welcomes contributors and users of different backgrounds and experience levels. Project discussions should be respectful, constructive, and focused on the work.

Examples of welcome behavior include giving actionable feedback, asking clarifying questions, acknowledging mistakes, and making room for others to participate. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

To report a conduct concern privately, email [nami.psg@gmail.com](mailto:nami.psg@gmail.com). Include links or context that help the maintainer understand the incident. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, and the maintainer will avoid sharing a reporter's identity without a clear need.
